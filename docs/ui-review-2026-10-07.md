# Usability and UI review — using it in the field

Reviewed 2026-10-07 after Run stage 19, from a walk through the app at
375 px (Playwright, the seed data, online and offline) and the code. The
lens: **a phone in one hand, on a train or at a check-in desk, maybe with
no signal and a low battery.** The earlier review
([ui_review.md](../ui_review.md), "find it fast") is done; this one picks
up where it left off.

## What's working

- **Today** answers the field questions: what's next, how long until, where
  I'm sleeping tonight, with Directions and the confirmation number one tap
  away (copy button included).
- **Offline is honest.** The amber bar says "Offline · saved copy from Oct 7,
  2:33 PM", and pages without a saved copy say so instead of failing. No
  "Network Error" toasts.
- **The map's offline fallback** turns into a list where every row still has
  Directions (they open the phone's maps app, which may have its own offline
  maps).
- **Entry pages** are big and readable: the time is the largest thing on
  the page, the confirmation is monospaced, and the time zone is said
  when it isn't the trip's ("Chicago time").
- **Journal writing works with no signal**, and photos wait for Wi-Fi.
- The dark theme is consistent, and the top bar's New memory button is
  always reachable.

## Field concerns, by priority

### 1. Documents aren't there when there's no signal (high)

The Documents page says "You're offline. Documents need a connection."
But you most need a boarding pass, passport scan or rail ticket at an
airport gate, a border or a station, which is often where there's no
roaming or Wi-Fi.

- **Recommend:** a "Keep on this phone" switch per document (or for all of
  them), saved in IndexedDB like the trip, so it opens offline. Leave
  passport scans opt-in: a lost phone that's unlocked would show them.
- An offline-available badge on each row, so you know before you need it.

### 2. Tonight's stay shows the check-in you've already done (high)

On day 3 of the Athens trip, Today's **Tonight** card says "Check-in Tue,
Oct 6 · 2:00 PM". By then the useful facts are:
- **while staying:** the check-out date and time;
- **on check-out day:** "Check out by 11:00 AM" at the top of Today, and
  the next stay (or the leg home) as Tonight.

### 3. No phone numbers (medium-high)

Stays and activities have an address, a link and a confirmation number,
but no phone. A late arrival, a missed restaurant booking or a lost
reservation all mean calling.
- **Recommend:** an optional `phone` on stays, activities and points of
  interest, shown as a `tel:` button next to Directions. Google Places
  returns `nationalPhoneNumber` / `internationalPhoneNumber`, so picking
  a place can fill it in.

### 4. Packing needs a connection (medium)

Packing is mostly done at home, but repacking at a hotel without Wi-Fi is
common. Save the list on the phone like the trip, and queue ticks the way
memories are queued.

### 5. Mobile data use (medium) — see the data-saver plan

Every trip page reloads the trip when the app comes back to the front.
Entry pages pull a Google photo and a live map, the map tab loads Google
tiles, and weather refreshes on each visit. On a capped roaming plan that
adds up. Your toggle idea is planned in
[run-20-data-saver.md](plan/run-20-data-saver.md).

### 6. Readability in sunlight (medium)

The app is dark only, and secondary text is mid-grey (`text-muted-
foreground`) on near-black. Outdoors at noon that's the hardest
combination to read.
- **Recommend:** a light theme following the phone's setting, or at least
  a "high contrast" switch that brightens muted text and borders. Pairs
  with the **text-size** preference already in the backlog.

### 7. Map framing hides pins at the edges (low-medium)

On the Bern trip, the Bern pin sits at the left edge behind the search
bar's corner, and the Wengen pins are on the right edge. Fit the bounds
with padding for the top controls (about 64 px) and the List button.

### 8. Repeated rows that read as duplicates (low)

- The offline map list shows "Chicago → Zürich" twice (the departure on
  May 10, the arrival on May 11). Label the second "Arrive · Zürich", as
  the day page already does.
- The trips list has "Okinawa & Taipei Trip — Fall 2026" and "Okinawa &
  Taipei — Fall 2026" (data, not code). Editing the trip's name is in the
  backlog, and deleting the old copy would help.

### 9. Tapping an active trip lands on Timeline, signing in lands on Today (low)

From the trips list, an active trip opens on Timeline. Signing in opens
Today. During a trip, Today is the better landing from both.

### 10. Viewers get a nearly empty drawer (low; your call in Run 17)

PriPri's drawer shows only All trips, Change password and Sign out.
Currency and Time zones never touch the server and hold no trip secrets,
and they're among the most useful tools on the road. Consider giving them
back to viewers.

### 11. Small visual things

- **Tool pages:** the Back button arrives with focus and looks pressed
  (filled grey) until you tap elsewhere. Use the focus ring for
  `:focus-visible` only.
- **Search results** show only the title. Add the time, and the place for
  activities ("10:00 AM · Varvakios Market"), so two "Dinner" rows can be
  told apart.
- **Add activity** is a long form in a dialog. On a phone a full-screen
  sheet with a sticky Save (as the entry page has) would scroll better.
- **The Admin table** scrolls sideways at 375 px to reach Analytics and
  Password. A card per user would fit (admin only, so low priority).

## Missing things you'd notice on a trip

- **Quick facts for a stay:** Wi-Fi password, door or locker code, host
  name. Today they go in notes. Copyable fields like the confirmation
  number would be faster.
- **Emergency card:** for each country on the trip, the emergency number
  (112, 911, 110/119…), the nearest embassy, the travel insurance policy
  and its phone. Saved offline.
- **"Leave by" for travel:** a leg's departure minus a buffer (airport
  2 h, train 15 min) shown on Today: "Leave by 7:40 for SWISS LX 9".
- **Expenses**, with the trip's currencies (the Currency tool already knows
  them): who paid and how much. A bigger feature, but the one families ask
  for most.
- **A "last updated" note on Today** when the trip changed on the server
  since you last looked ("Julian edited Day 4"). With several editors, a
  change you didn't make can surprise you.
