## Appendix — Getting Around Taipei

### Recommended approach for this stay
Walk within neighborhoods, use the **MRT** between neighborhoods, and use a taxi when luggage, rain or fatigue makes it worthwhile. Zhongshan Station is the hotel's nearby transit hub, connecting the **Red (R11)** and **Green (G14)** lines.

### EasyCard and MRT basics
- Buy **one EasyCard per person** and add stored value. It works on Taipei MRT, city buses and Taoyuan Airport MRT.
- On the MRT, **tap in and tap out** with the same card. On city buses, **tap when boarding and when getting off**.
- Current Taipei MRT single-journey fares are **NT$20–65**, depending on distance; airport travel has a separate fare.
- Top up at MRT stations or participating convenience stores; carrying cash makes top-ups straightforward.
- Follow line colors, station names and terminal directions. Check the train's destination when branches or short-turn services appear.
- Use the official route map or Google Maps for current routing, transfers and last trains.

### Useful lines from your Zhongshan base

| Line | Useful destinations / approach |
|---|---|
| **Red — Tamsui–Xinyi** | Direct from Zhongshan to Taipei Main Station, Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall, Dongmen / Yongkang Street, Da'an and Taipei 101. For Beitou hot springs, travel north to Beitou and transfer to the Xinbeitou branch. |
| **Green — Songshan–Xindian** | Direct from Zhongshan to Beimen, Ximen and Songshan for Raohe Night Market. Beimen is useful for approaching southern Dadaocheng on foot. |
| **Blue — Bannan** | Transfer at Taipei Main Station or Ximen for Longshan Temple and eastern Taipei. |
| **Orange — Zhonghe–Xinlu** | Useful for other parts of Zhongshan, including Xingtian Temple; transfer as needed. |

### Buses, taxis and bicycles
- **Buses:** Useful for places beyond an MRT station, including the National Palace Museum. Use route directions and tap EasyCard both on and off; choose the correct stop and direction.
- **Taxis:** A practical backup for luggage or a late, rainy evening. Show the hotel's Chinese name/address saved on your phone. Ask the concierge to arrange a pickup when helpful.
- **YouBike:** An optional way to explore parks and river paths. Check current visitor registration and payment requirements before relying on it; an EasyCard alone does not complete registration.

### Airport transfers — Taoyuan (TPE)
- **Airport MRT:** Express trains connect Terminal 1 (**A12**) with Taipei Main Station (**A1**) in roughly **35 minutes**. This is train time, excluding immigration, baggage collection, station walks and hotel travel.
- From A1, follow signs to Taipei Metro, take the Red Line one stop north from Taipei Main Station to **Zhongshan**, then walk to the hotel. The Airport MRT and city MRT platforms are separate and require a walk between them.
- With suitcases, a taxi from the A1 area to the hotel can simplify the final leg; a direct airport taxi or prearranged hotel transfer is another option.
- For the **Nov 12, 6:20 PM departure**, work backward from Tigerair's check-in requirements and allow extra transfer time. Confirm the airport departure plan closer to travel.
